Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a refund if:

  • Technical issues on our end prevent you from accessing the platform for over 7 consecutive days.
  • You purchased the wrong plan and notify us within 3 days (72 hours) of payment.
  • The agreed-upon initial onboarding support was not provided within the promised timeframe.

You are not eligible for a refund if:

  • You change your mind after the 3-day window has passed.
  • You have already accessed premium training materials or AI specialized tools.
  • Your business model changes or you decide to stop your operations.

2. Refund Process & Timeline

Email refunds@velorixai.com within 72 hours of your transaction with your Name, Registered Email, and Transaction ID. We review all requests within 3 business days. If approved, the amount will be credited to your original payment method within 7-10 business days.

3. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your dashboard. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.
